This notice explains what personal data this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to meet the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). The other Pen of Wonder sites are covered by the [main privacy notice]({world:main/privacy}), which says the same things.

## Who we are

Pen of Wonder Research is the research service of Pen of Wonder Consults, a registered business name in Nigeria, founded by Wonderful Adebagbo and based in Ibadan, Oyo State. Pen of Wonder Consults is the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at {email}.

## What we collect

**When you use the contact form:** your name, email address, which of the three options describes you (researcher, editing firm or student), your message, any file link you choose to include, and a record that you ticked the consent box. The form also records the time it was sent and how long it took to fill in, which helps us tell people from spam robots.

**To stop spam,** our form system counts how many messages arrive from one internet connection in an hour. It keeps only a scrambled code (a hash) made from your IP address and your browser's name, never the address or browser details themselves, and deletes the counts after a day.

**When you message us on WhatsApp or by email:** whatever you choose to send, including your phone number on WhatsApp.

**When you visit the site:** anonymous, aggregated statistics about page views (such as which pages are read, the referring site, browser and country), collected by Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies or local storage and does not identify you.

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies, so there is no cookie banner. If you use the light or dark switch, your choice is saved in your own browser’s storage so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us.

## Why, and the lawful basis

- **To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote or a free review.** Lawful basis: your consent, given with the box on the form, and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
- **To provide the service if you go ahead.** Lawful basis: performance of a contract.
- **To keep basic records** such as invoices. Lawful basis: legal obligation.
- **To keep the form free of spam and to understand how the site is used, in aggregate.** Lawful basis: legitimate interests. No individual is identified.

We don’t sell your data, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list unless you sign up for one yourself.

## Who processes it

- **Cloudflare** hosts this website, provides the anonymous analytics, and runs our own form system. Messages sent through that system are stored in a database we control on Cloudflare, which may process data outside Nigeria.
- **Formspree** received contact form messages before our own system, and forwards any it still receives to our email. Formspree is based in the United States. We stop using it once the move is complete, and this notice will say so.
- **Telegram** delivers an alert to us when a form message arrives: your name, email address, which option describes you, and the first line of your message. Telegram is based outside Nigeria and the UK.
- **Google (Gmail)** hosts our email.
- **WhatsApp (Meta)**, if you message us there.

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria and the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards these providers use for international transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

Form messages in our own database are deleted 24 months after our last contact with you. Formspree keeps form submissions for 30 days on the plan we use. Telegram alerts are deleted from our chat after 12 months. Messages in our email and WhatsApp are kept for 12 months, then deleted, unless we have worked together, in which case we keep what we need for our records and tax for as long as the law requires.

Manuscripts and files you share with us are used only for your work and deleted when the work is finished, unless you ask us to keep them.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct anything that is wrong;
- delete your data;
- restrict or object to how we use it;
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything we did before;
- give you your data in a portable format.

Email {email} to use any of these rights. We’ll reply within one month.

## Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to:

- in the UK, the **Information Commissioner’s Office** (ICO): [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/);
- in Nigeria, the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** (NDPC): [ndpc.gov.ng](https://ndpc.gov.ng/).
