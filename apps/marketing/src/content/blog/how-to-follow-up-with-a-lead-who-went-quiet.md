## Does a lead going quiet always mean the same thing?

No. Sometimes they're not a good fit right now. Sometimes their objections weren't handled properly in the conversation or at the close.

That's why I don't follow up on everyone.

## When should you follow up with a lead who went quiet?

I follow up when three things are true:

1. I qualified them properly.
2. I cleared their objections.
3. I saw real commitment, maybe even a promise to pay that day that never came.

If a lead meets all three and still goes quiet, I follow up. If they don't, a follow-up won't fix the real problem. Getting the first conversation right matters more, which is covered in [how to reply to a new lead from your ads](/blog/how-to-reply-to-a-new-lead-from-whatsapp-ads).

## What is the 3-7-7 follow-up pattern?

This is how I do it, from my own experience. I borrowed the timing from cold email, a 3, 7, 7 pattern, and I use it beyond email too.

| Follow-up | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | 3 days after the conversation where we closed | Reminds them of the value: what the solution solves and what their life looks like without it. Makes an offer that makes a quick decision easier. Asks if they have any concerns or need more information. |
| Second | 7 days after the first (day 10) | Makes the case again, this time using what they told me in our conversation, so they can see why this is a good fit for them. |
| Third | 7 days after the second (day 17) | The closing follow-up. Leaves the door open. |

That puts the three follow-ups on day 3, day 10 and day 17.

## What should the last follow-up say?

The third one is the closing message. Life gets in the way, and I don't treat that as a problem. I leave the door open. Here is the message I've used:

> Hello Sir. Good morning, Sir. I know things get busy. I'm going to close out our conversation on my end. If you want to pick this up at some point, just reach out, and we can talk. No pressure either way.

Adjust the greeting to the person, but keep the shape: acknowledge they're busy, close the conversation on your side, and invite them to come back with no pressure.

## Why does a follow-up fail?

None of this works if the groundwork isn't done. A follow-up can't fix poor qualifying or objections that were never answered. If many of your leads go quiet, look at the qualifying question in your first reply and at how objections are handled on the call, before adding more follow-ups.
