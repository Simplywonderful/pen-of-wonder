## What is the 4-day offer test?

It's a quick way to find out whether an offer has any pull before you put serious money behind it. I picked it up at a one-hour session called House of Scaling, on scaling ads.

It also changed how I think about creatives. Until then I believed ads had to be video, and nothing else. In this session video didn't come up at all. The test uses graphic designs.

## How do you run it?

1. **Start from winners.** Take graphic designs that have already won before.
2. **Adapt them to your offer.** Change them to fit the offer and the product you're selling, and make as many variations as you can.
3. **Set up a sales campaign.** Test different copy variations against different designs, using a campaign budget so the copy and the designs are tested together.
4. **Run it for four days.** Then apply the kill-or-scale rule below.

## What is the kill-or-scale rule?

> **4 days**: The test window from the session. No sale on a product under ₦30,000 means kill it. At least one sale means it can be scaled.

| After four days | Product price | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No sale | Under ₦30,000 | Kill it |
| At least one sale | Any | A sign it can be scaled and improved |

The ₦30,000 figure is the one given in the session, and this is the rule I found most useful.

## Does this replace letting ads run for 10 to 15 days?

No. My own rule is still to [let ads run 10 to 15 days before judging them](/blog/how-long-to-let-a-meta-ad-run-before-judging-it). This is a different test: a short one to find out whether an offer has any pull at all, not a way to judge a full campaign. I'm also not dropping video. If you want the thinking on when video or images fit, read [why choosing image, video or carousel is a strategy decision](/blog/ad-format-follows-audience-temperature).

## Why test the offer before spending big?

Because ads amplify what already works. An offer nobody has paid for is expensive to learn about at full budget, which is the same reasoning behind [not paying for ads until you've sold the thing without them](/blog/dont-pay-for-ads-until-youve-sold-without-them). A sales campaign needs correct tracking to read the result, so check [how a sales campaign differs from a leads campaign](/blog/sales-campaigns-vs-leads-campaigns-meta-pixel-tracking) before you launch one.

## What I don't know yet

I haven't run this test, so I don't know how it plays out in practice, including whether four days is enough at your budget. When I run it I'll share what happened.
